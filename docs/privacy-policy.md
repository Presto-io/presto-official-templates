# Privacy Policy

Presto and the Presto Official Templates are designed to run locally on the user's device.

## Data collection

Presto Official Templates do not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

Template executables process local Markdown input and produce Typst output through standard input and standard output. They do not include telemetry, analytics, advertising identifiers, account tracking, or background reporting.

## Network access

Official template executables do not require network access to convert documents. The template release workflow and public registry distribute template binaries and metadata, but the template executables themselves are local document conversion tools.

## Local files

Templates only receive the document content that Presto passes to them for conversion. They do not upload local files or send document content to external services.

## Contact

For privacy or security questions about Presto Official Templates, open an issue in the public project repository:

https://github.com/Presto-io/presto-official-templates/issues
